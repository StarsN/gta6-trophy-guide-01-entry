    The road to Vice Got a Price!

    Five stages, in the order that makes the platinum shortest. Stage times are pacing estimates built on published roadmap reporting of roughly 200 hours for a full completion (PowerPyx, cited on the Sources tab); the order itself is built from the official trophy descriptions — story first, because activities, properties and robbery targets unlock as the chapters advance, and the one-off moments flagged missable all live inside the story pass.

    Missable trophies — read before you play

    The official descriptions promise no chapter select and no mission replay, so any trophy tied to a single story moment, a single location visit or a single choice is treated here as missable until earned. Five trophies carry that flag; each card below quotes the official description the flag is built from, so you can judge the reasoning yourself.

    Sources & method

    Every guide in this app is written for this entry, in our own words. Facts are drawn from the sources below and each trophy's guide names its sources beside it — a cited guide beats an uncited one, however detailed. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official ones, shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them.

    How the roadmap and missable flags were built

    The roadmap order and the five missable flags are derived from the official descriptions themselves: chapter and named-mission trophies define the story spine; strand-final trophies define the stranger stage; counted trophies (250 stunts, 42 shooting-range challenges, 100 photo spots, 3 card sets) define the grinds and sweeps; and any description that names a single moment, place or choice — an unscheduled departure at VCIA, a cocktail on Ocean Beach, a showroom escape, a bowling follow-through, a decision made “for better or worse” — is flagged missable, because the list nowhere promises a way back.

    The PS5 list contains no online trophies: all 55 are offline, so the Online filter on the checklist honestly reads zero. Progress is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back at any time. This app loads nothing from the network and works fully offline.
